## Description (4,000)
SunClock is a clock for the sky. Instead of hours and minutes, it shows where you are in
the solar day: how long since sunrise, how long until sunset, and what the sun and moon
are doing right now.

THE SOLAR DIAL
• A 24-hour dial with solar noon at the top and solar midnight at the bottom.
• The ring is colored by how high the sun really is: night, the three twilights, golden
  hour and full day, blending smoothly for your location and date.
• The sun moves around the ring through the day, reddening at the horizon. Inside it, the
  moon follows its own day, drawn in its real phase from NASA imagery.
• In the center: "1h 48m after solar noon", or a 12- or 24-hour clock with solar time.

SOLAR ALARMS
• Set alarms relative to any solar event: "30 min before sunrise", "1 hr after solar
  noon", "civil dusk".
• Repeat on chosen days. Because sunrise moves every day, SunClock recalculates your
  alarms for each day and wherever you are.
• Real system alarms: they ring in silent mode and Focus, with Stop and Snooze on the
  Lock Screen.

EVERY EVENT, EVERY DAY
• Astronomical, nautical and civil dawn and dusk; sunrise, solar noon, sunset and solar
  midnight.
• Moonrise, moonset, the moon's highest point and its phase.
• Midnight sun and polar night handled properly.

WIDGETS AND APPLE WATCH
• Sun Ring widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen, including a medium widget with a
  live countdown to the next sunrise or sunset.
• Apple Watch complications for your favorite faces, a Smart Stack card, and a Watch app
  where you turn the Digital Crown to see where the sun and moon will be later.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything is calculated on your device. No account, no tracking, no ads, no network
  requests. Your location never leaves your phone.
• Prefer not to share location? Enter a place by hand.

Sun and moon positions use the NOAA solar algorithm and a lunar model checked against an
independent ephemeris to within a minute. Moon imagery: NASA Scientific Visualization
Studio (LRO).
