# SunClock Support

**Report a problem or request a feature:**
[open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/KramerLewis/circadian/issues).

## Common questions

**Why does an alarm not ring on some days?**
In polar regions some events don't happen on some days (for example, there's no sunrise
during polar night). The alarm list shows when the next one will be.

**My alarms are at the wrong time after travelling.**
SunClock recalculates alarms when you open it, and in the background when iOS allows.
Open the app once after arriving somewhere new.

**Can I use SunClock without sharing my location?**
Yes. Tap the location button on the Sun tab and enter a manual location.

**What is "solar time"?**
Time as a sundial would show it: 12:00 is when the sun is highest (solar noon), which
is usually not 12:00 on your clock.

See also the [privacy policy](privacy.md).
