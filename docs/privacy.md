# SunClock Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 22, 2026_

SunClock shows time relative to the sun and sets alarms for solar events. It is
designed to work without collecting anything about you.

## What SunClock uses, and where it stays

- **Location.** With your permission, SunClock reads your approximate location to
  calculate sunrise, sunset, twilight and solar time. All calculations happen on your
  device. Your location is never sent to us or to anyone else. The last known location
  is stored on your device so the app and its widgets work before a new fix arrives.
- **Manual location.** If you enter a location by hand, it is stored on your device
  only.
- **Alarms and settings.** Your alarms and settings are stored on your device. Alarms
  are scheduled with Apple's system alarm service (AlarmKit) on your device.
- **Widgets.** The app shares its current location with its own widgets through a
  storage area on your device that only SunClock can access.

## What SunClock does not do

- It does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data.
- It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
- It makes no network requests.

## Your choices

You can turn location access off at any time in **Settings › SunClock › Location**
and use a manual location instead. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your
device.

## Changes

If a future version adds features that change this (for example, optional syncing
between devices), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and
the feature will be optional.
