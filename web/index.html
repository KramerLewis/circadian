SunClock

Your day, on the sun's clock

SunClock draws today as a 24-hour dial with solar noon at the top: daylight, every twilight, the moon, and special sky days. Everything is computed in your browser; your location never leaves it.

In 3D

Sunlight comes from the sun's direction; the moon's lit side is its phase. Your place turns with Earth. Drag to look around (toward the sun to see the night side), twist two fingers to roll, tap the compass to put north back up, double-click to reset.
